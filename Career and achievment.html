Cristiano Ronaldo

Club career history

1992–1995: Andorinha (Youth)

1995–1997: Nacional (Youth)

1997–2002: Sporting CP (Youth)

2002–2003: Sporting CP

2003–2009: Manchester United

2009–2018: Real Madrid

2018–2021: Juventus

2021–2022: Manchester United

2023–Present: Al-Nassr FC

Major achievment

Ballon d'Or: 5-time winner (2008, 2013, 2014, 2016, 2017)

European Golden Shoe: 4-time winner

UEFA Champions League: 5 titles (1 with Man Utd, 4 with Real Madrid)

World Records: All-time top international male goalscorer, and the first male player to feature in six consecutive FIFA World Cups up to 2026.
